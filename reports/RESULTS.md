## Late-delivery risk models

Evaluation is order-grouped: 5 folds over `order_id`, so no order is on both sides. Fold 0 is the train/test split (88,151 / 22,038 items; 77,178 / 19,292 orders); the five folds together give the out-of-fold scores stored in `LatePredictions`. Base rate of late items 7.91% on the test fold. Source: `reports/model_metrics.json`, `reports/oof_metrics.json`, `models/registry.json`.

| model | role | test AUC (fold 0) | out-of-fold AUC | fold mean ± std | AUC on its own training rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

At the default cut-off the primary model flags 52% of late items (recall 0.52) with precision 0.19. `late_risk` is a ranking score, not a probability: with balanced class weights its median (0.33) is far above the 7.9% late rate.
